# Privacy Policy for JSON LD Visual Editor

**Last updated: July 2026**

Antinna ("us", "we", or "our") operates the **JSON LD Visual Editor** mobile application. This Privacy Policy informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our app.

---

### 1. Information Collection and Use
We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personally identifiable information (PII) or personal data.
* **Offline Storage:** The JSON LD Visual Editor runs completely offline on your device. Any documents, specifications, or metadata you create, edit, or import are stored locally on your device's private Drift database. No data is ever sent to any external servers or third parties.
* **No Tracking:** We do not use third-party tracking, analytics SDKs, or advertising networks.

### 2. Network Connections
Our application makes a secure, single external network request to `schema.org` to dynamically fetch the latest semantic schema specifications. This connection is used solely to keep your local specification database up-to-date. No personal details, unique identifiers, or usage statistics are transmitted during this spec synchronization.

### 3. Links to Other Sites
Our application contains links to external sites (such as GitHub, YouTube, Instagram, X/Twitter, Facebook, Substack, and search.google.com) that are not operated by us. We advise you to review the Privacy Policy of every site you visit.

### 4. Children's Privacy
Our Service does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

### 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted within this app.
